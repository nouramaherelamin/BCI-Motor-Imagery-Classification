BCI Motor Imagery Classifier

Classifies whether a person is imagining LEFT or RIGHT hand movement from EEG alone. Submit one raw 10-second trial (4 channels: FZ, C3, CZ, C4 at 250 Hz) and the trained model returns its prediction with class probabilities.
